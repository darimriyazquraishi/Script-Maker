If START.bat says llama-server.exe was not found even though it exists in:
F:\ScriptMaker_OneTap_Handoff\llama\llama-server.exe

Use this fixed START.bat from this package.

The previous launcher could overwrite the detected llama-server path with a blank
LLAMA_SERVER_EXE= setting from .env. This fixed version ignores blank settings.

Expected layout:
ScriptMaker_OneTap_Handoff\
  START.bat
  INSTALL.bat
  llama\
    llama-server.exe
  models\
    Qwen3-14B-Q4_K_M.gguf
